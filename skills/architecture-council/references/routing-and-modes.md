# Routing and Modes

## Quick Council
Use three relevant professional reviewers for an important but reversible decision. Omit cross-examination.

## Duo Review
Use two opposing professional lenses when one core tension defines the decision. Require direct response and self-correction.

## Full Council
Use all six professional reviewers plus the Independent Chairman for high-impact, difficult-to-reverse, cross-functional, or policy-level decisions. Require blind openings, anonymized cross-examination, final stances, weighted tally, and synthesis.

Choose the smallest mode that can still expose a decision-changing disagreement.
