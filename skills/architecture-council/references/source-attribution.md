## Adapted concepts

- full, quick, and duo modes;
- problem restatement gate;
- independent opening positions;
- direct challenge and self-correction;
- evidence labels;
- method diversity;
- domain-weight seat selected before analysis;
- confidence-weighted tally;
- independent chair;
- minority preservation;
- acceptable compromises;
- kill criteria;
- one concrete next action;
- outcome tracking.

## Material changes

The ChatGPT adaptation:

- uses professional executive and architecture roles by default;
- makes historical lenses optional;
- supports honest single-model structured deliberation;
- removes required local CLI and provider-detection dependencies from the core Skill;
- adds Mornex and customer-sensitive data handling controls;
- adds a formal boundary with Lessons Learned;
- adds JSON dossier and decision-record validation.
