## Legacy mode rules

- Preserve the same decision dossier, evidence labels, mode selection, dissent policy, weighted tally, kill criteria, and outcome tracking used by the professional council.
- Select lenses for method diversity.
- Do not use biographical claims as evidence.
- Do not imitate distinctive personal speech patterns.
- State that the positions are model-generated analytical lenses.
- Keep the independent chair separate from the voting panel.

## Mapping to professional roles

Legacy lenses may supplement, but should not replace, a required professional review when a decision involves real architecture, security, compliance, contractual, or delivery accountability.
