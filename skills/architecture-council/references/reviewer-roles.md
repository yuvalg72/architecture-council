# Professional Reviewer Roles

## Strategic and Business Reviewer
Evaluate business value, priorities, return, strategic alignment, opportunity cost, and long-term impact.

## Technical and Security Architect
Evaluate technical correctness, security, supportability, integration, lifecycle, resilience, and technical debt.

## Delivery and PMO Reviewer
Evaluate scope, dependencies, ownership, sequencing, timeline, resources, acceptance, rollback readiness, and closure.

## Risk and Governance Reviewer
Evaluate risk, compliance, auditability, decision rights, controls, residual exposure, and rollback governance.

## Operational Simplicity Reviewer
Evaluate whether the solution is practical, maintainable, understandable, supportable, and no more complex than necessary.

## Customer and Stakeholder Reviewer
Evaluate customer impact, communication, commercial commitments, responsibility split, usability, and stakeholder alignment.

## Independent Chairman
Synthesize only. Do not vote. Preserve dissent, expose unresolved questions, verify the weighted tally, define kill criteria, and issue one immediate next action.

## Panel design principles
- Select for useful friction, not popularity.
- Prefer complementary lenses over many similar reviewers.
- Include the reviewer most likely to expose a blind spot.
- Assign the domain-weight seat before positions exist.
- Keep the Chairman independent from the voting panel.
