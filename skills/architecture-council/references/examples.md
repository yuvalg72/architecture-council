# Examples

## Quick Council

**Prompt**

```text
Use the Architecture Council in Quick mode to decide whether a reversible pilot should use the existing platform or a separate temporary environment. Focus on security, delivery effort, and operational support.
```

**Expected panel**

- Technical and Security Architect
- Delivery and PMO Reviewer
- Operational Simplicity Reviewer
- Independent Chair

## Duo Review

**Prompt**

```text
Run a Duo Review on standardization versus local flexibility for branch network designs. Define the conditions under which each side should prevail.
```

**Expected polarity**

- Technical and Security Architect for standardization
- Customer and Stakeholder Reviewer or Operational Simplicity Reviewer for controlled flexibility

## Full Council

**Prompt**

```text
Convene the Full Architecture Council to evaluate a new organization-wide remote access architecture. Compare at least three options, preserve the minority view, define kill criteria, and provide one immediate next action.
```

**Expected output**

- complete decision dossier;
- all six professional reviewer positions;
- anonymized cross-examination;
- weighted tally;
- recommendation or split;
- minority position;
- kill criteria;
- outcome checkpoint.

## Pre-mortem instead of council

**Prompt**

```text
The decision is already approved. Do not rerun the council. Perform a pre-mortem and identify the highest-risk implementation assumptions.
```

Use a direct risk review rather than presenting the approved decision as undecided.
