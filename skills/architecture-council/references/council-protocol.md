# Council Protocol

1. Ground the question with a validated Decision Dossier.
2. Select Quick, Duo, or Full mode.
3. Select reviewers before positions exist.
4. Restate the problem through each lens.
5. Produce independent opening positions.
6. Challenge assumptions and require self-correction.
7. Record final stances in the required machine-readable format.
8. Calculate confidence-weighted support.
9. Preserve the strongest minority case and unresolved questions.
10. Let the Independent Chairman synthesize without voting.
11. Define observable kill criteria and exactly one next action.
12. Record a prediction and review checkpoint before acting.

Do not use deliberation to decorate a decision already made.
